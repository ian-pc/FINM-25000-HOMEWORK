3. Questions:
Comment on what this regression tells us about the nature of interest rates.
That is, can we forecast the next period’s rate?
Does the series have autocorrelation?

Answer:
SOFR is highly persistent and has positive autocorrelation. 
A high R-squared and beta close to 1 indicate that the previous period's rate helps forecast the next period's rate, although the forecast is not perfect.

4. Questions:
Are SPY returns autocorrelated? And SPY prices?

Answer:
SPY Returns vs. Prices:
SPY daily returns generally have little autocorrelation. A beta and R-squared close to zero indicate that yesterday's return provides little information about today's return. 
SPY prices, however, are highly autocorrelated, with beta close to 1 and a high R-squared. Prices are persistent, but daily returns are much harder to predict.
